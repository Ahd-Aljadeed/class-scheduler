💡 How to Use It (The Right Way)

To get the most accurate and optimized schedule, you need to feed the system all available options:

  1. Add all your classes and sections: If you are taking Calculus and Computer Skills, and each course has 3 different sections, you need to enter all 6 sections into the system.
  2. Let the math work: Once your options are in, the website will crunch the numbers and calculate the absolute best schedule combination for you.
  3. Plan with friends: Add a friend from the people bar to give them their own tab, then turn on the friends overlay to find shared classes and breaks.
That’s it. Dive in and enjoy!
